Northfield Outdoor Co. at a three-day music festival

Three ways to put 30,000 festival-goers in Northfield gear for the first cold night.
June 2027 · 40 × 30 ft footprint near the main stage · Budget $150–250K
The brief

Make Northfield the brand people thank when the desert gets cold.

Audience

30,000 a day, mostly 22–38, camping on site, outdoorsy but not gear experts.

Place

Outdoor, 40 × 30 ft, open 11 am to 2 am, generator power, wind over 20 mph most evenings.

Success looks like

45,000 interactions, 8,000 email sign-ups, and the jacket in festival photos.

Assumptions: three-day load-in, union crew not required, sampling of product allowed.

Concept B · Stretch · Guest journey

The Warming Hut: a lantern-lit cabin that opens when the temperature drops.

  1. Arrive. A glowing A-frame cabin, a live thermometer on the roof.
  2. Enter. Below 60°F the doors open; inside is warm, quiet and wood-lined.
  3. Try on. Grab a Northfield fleece from the wall and wear it for the night.
  4. Share. The window seat frames the main stage lights behind you.
  5. Leave with. Return the fleece for a patch, or buy it at 30% off.

Why it fits Northfield

Their "Stay Out Longer" campaign is about the hours after dark. This is that, live.

Throughput

720 guests/hour (60 inside × 5 min dwell)
