Idea 1 · Signature experience

R2 Fog Camp: a campsite between stages, set up the way R2 owners camp.

How it works

  1. Six R2s parked as a campsite, with rooftop tents and travel kitchens.
  2. Climb up, sit in, charge your phone, and get out of the fog for a few minutes.
  3. Coffee is poured from a tailgate, as Rivian did at SXSW.
  4. Scan to book a demo drive at a Rivian Space the week after the festival.

What fans get

A warm seat, a hot drink and the best photo spot in the park.

Idea 2 · Getting there

The city asks fans not to drive in. Make the trip there and home part of Rivian.

Bike valet by Rivian

The festival already runs bike valets and shuttles. Rivian presents them, with e-bike demo rides, as it did with ALSO at SXSW.

Wikipedia; DesignRush, Mar 2026

The ride home

Branded shuttle stops and a lit wayfinding trail to Muni, which the city adds service for during the festival.

ABC7, Aug 2026

Why it works

Every fan passes through it twice a day, and it shows the brand solving a real problem instead of adding noise.
