03Scope by phase

Discovery (week 1). Kickoff and tasting at the roastery, a shelf audit of 20 competing brands, and a one-page strategy brief you approve before design starts.

Identity concepts (weeks 2–3). Three distinct directions shown on bags, cups and a storefront. One feedback session; you pick one direction to refine.

Identity system (weeks 3–4). The chosen direction refined into the final logo suite, palette and type, documented in the guidelines. Two rounds of revisions.

Packaging (weeks 4–6). Bags for three coffees in two sizes, print-ready files, and review of printed proofs with your printer.
