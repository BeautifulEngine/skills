03Scope by phase

Discovery (week 1). Kickoff with the partnerships team, a review of the 2026 recap and assets, research on the six target brands, and a one-page creative brief you approve.

Pitch framework (weeks 1–2). A master deck built from the festival's brand guidelines and photography, plus a slide library the team can reuse. One review session, two rounds of revisions.

Brand decks (weeks 3–5). Six decks in two batches of three, each built around that brand's audience and goals, with speaker notes and a leave-behind.

Concept renderings (weeks 5–6). Three renderings of the ideas the team wants to lead with. Stock and 3D assets billed at cost.
