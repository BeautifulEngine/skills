Cream is the page background, not white. Coral is the accent for buttons, links and the eyebrow line; at 3.1:1 on white it suits large text and shapes, and reads fully on Ink. The hero sits on a sunset gradient of coral, teal and sand.

03Logo

A "BE" monogram in cream on a Coral circle, followed by a "BEAUTIFUL ENGINE" wordmark in condensed caps in Ink. It sits on Cream in a rounded, frosted nav bar. Official files: ask Jordan Robinson via beautifulengine.com.

04Interface

See what I've madeResuméPill buttons (999 px radius) · primary has a soft Coral glow · 1.5 px outline for secondary

Project cards are styled like vintage postcards with a stamp and postmark in the corner, tilted and overlapping. Small Coral eyebrows in spaced caps sit above headlines, led by a short Coral rule. Navigation is a floating pill bar with a Coral outline "Say hi" button.

05Voice

Sounds like

  • Hands-on: “Visual storyteller who builds”
  • Specific: “Pitch design, brand systems and experiential work for Sphere, MSG and Twitter.”
  • Warm and casual: “Say hi →” and “Talk soon.”

Avoids

  • Corporate phrasing like “Contact us” or “Solutions”
  • Long paragraphs: short lines, first person
  • Abstract claims without the work beside them

06Imagery

Real project photography (venues, stunts, launches) framed as postcards with stamps and postmarks, laid over an illustrated sunset landscape in Coral, Teal and Sand. Warm, nostalgic and travel-themed: each project is a place you've been sent a card from.
