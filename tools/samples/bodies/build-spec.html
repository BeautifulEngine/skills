06Build steps

  1. Tower: cut panels, glue and screw the 2×2 frame. Check: square within 1/16 in before the back goes on.
  2. Letters: laser the faces and returns, bond with acrylic cement. Check: dry-fit every letter in its cutout.
  3. LEDs: lay strip inside each letter box, solder joins, heat-shrink. Check: test every letter before sealing.
  4. Finish: prime and paint the tower, two coats. Check: 24 h cure before install.

07Safety

Before you build
  • Cut acrylic only: never laser PVC or vinyl. Run the laser with ventilation and stay with it.
  • An 8 ft sign catches wind: ballast with at least 100 lb and strap it to a fixed point; take it down above 25 mph.
  • Fuse the battery output and keep all wiring 12 V DC.
