A honeycomb planter that stacks and clicks together. Start with one cell for your desk and grow it into a wall of succulents.

  • Prints without supports on any FDM printer
  • Built-in drip tray with a drain gap, so roots don't sit in water
  • Cells lock side by side with a dovetail and stack two high

What's included: 2 STL files (pot, tray), a 3MF with a 6-cell plate ready to slice, and a README with settings. This is a digital file; no physical item ships.
