Paloma Park Sessions: selling out a sunset

A free summer concert series was losing its crowd to a new festival across town. We rebuilt it around the one thing nobody else could offer, the 30 minutes before sunset, and sold out all 12 nights.

ClientPaloma Park Sessions
RoleCreative lead: brand, campaign, on-site
Team4 designers, 1 producer, 2 partners
DisciplinesBrand, experiential, social
Year2026

ChallengeSame park, same bands, a shrinking crowd

Attendance had dropped 22% in two years, and a new paid festival had booked three of the series' regular acts. The budget for the season was $180K, with nothing extra for talent.

InsightPeople came for the sky, not the lineup

In 1,400 tagged posts from the previous summer, 71% were taken in the half hour before sunset, and fewer than 1 in 10 named a band. The series already owned the best view in the city; it had just never said so.

IdeaEvery show is a sunset show

We moved set times so the headliner always plays into sunset, printed each night's sunset time on the poster instead of the lineup, and turned the last 30 minutes into a ritual the crowd could join.

Process and craftA system that changes color every week

The identity is a single striped sun. Its colors come from a sunset photo taken at the park the week before each show, so 12 nights got 12 palettes from one set of rules. The hard part was print lead times: we locked the layout and set color last, two days before each run.

On site, a 30-second amber light cue starts the last half hour, and the crowd's phone lights close the night. Partners joined through the ritual rather than banners.
