ATHLOS: pitching a property with no history yet

ATHLOS, Alexis Ohanian's women's track and field event, came to Crown Properties Collection needing sponsorship pitches fast. We built one pitch framework from its brand and photography, then made decks for L'Oréal, Google Pixel, Airbnb, Ramp and 20 more brands, some overnight.

ClientATHLOS
StudioCrown Properties Collection (OVG)
RoleDesign Director
TeamTwo designers, with coordinators and directors
Year2025–2026, NYC

ChallengeA new client, tight timelines, a different ask from every brand

ATHLOS came to CPC as a new client that needed sponsorship pitches on tight timelines. Each brand we pitched wanted something different, so for each one we had to work out what would land and why it should sign on for an ATHLOS event.

InsightSell the event, not the numbers

A new property has no ratings or history to sell, so each pitch had to show the brand inside an event worth being part of.

IdeaOne framework, so the hours go into the ideas

I set up a pitch framework from the ATHLOS brand guidelines and photography. For each brand we pitched a few ideas built around its audience and goals, in decks that paired ATHLOS branding with a nod to the client's own.

ResultsFaster decks, more ideas, sponsorship business closed
